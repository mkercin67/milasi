# MiLaSi v3.2 release notes (DRAFT)

Status: draft for the owner. Not final until the owner has signed off every fixture against its scan.

## New
- Ensembles: add and remove parts and staves, brackets and braces, templates (SATB, piano, voice and piano, string quartet).
- Clefs: C clefs (soprano to baritone), octave-shift clefs, clef changes inside a staff (also mid-measure).
- Notation: common and cut time, fermata, breath mark, caesura, trill, mordent, turn, grace notes, tuplets (3:2), hairpins.
- Lyrics: up to eight verses, stacked, with hyphens and extenders.
- Playback: one MIDI channel per part, mute and solo per staff.
- Layout fix: dynamics with tall letters (f, ff, mf) and dynamics under low notes or accents no longer overprint them.

## Checked against public-domain or CC0 editions
Beethoven Op. 18 No. 1 (mm. 1-8), Bach BWV 140/7 (mm. 1-6, SATB and keyboard), Schubert D. 547 (mm. 1-5), Mozart K. 545 (mm. 1-7), Bach BWV 772 (mm. 1-7), Breidenbach, Méditation (mm. 13-21 and 148-153, CC0).
The checks found and fixed real defects (dynamic overlap, an octave error in an entered fixture).

## Not yet
Rehearsal marks, text expressions, bowing signs, cross-part selection, pages and print layout (M7d, M8), MusicXML (M9). See `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` and `docs/design/feature-parity.md`.
