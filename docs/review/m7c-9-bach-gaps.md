# M7c-9b Bach BWV 140/7 (mm. 1-6): differences and open points

Source: IMSLP #948558 (Toru Yaskawa, Public Domain dedication). All claims UNVERIFIED until the owner checks them against the scan.

- The edition is a two-staff keyboard layout (soprano+alto, tenor+bass). The fixture splits it into four SATB staves; the stems tell which voice owns which note.
- The edition has no lyrics; none are entered.
- Fermatas: the scan puts one above the upper staff and one below the lower staff; the fixture puts one above each of the four voices.
- Open: m. 4 beat 4 bass is read as Eb4 (a notehead shared with the tenor, with an up stem and a down stem). Check the scan.
- Open: m. 2 tenor beat 4 is A natural (natural sign in the scan); m. 6 bass last eighth is Db3 (flat sign).
- Ties: bass m. 1 (half to eighth) and alto m. 5 (quarter to eighth).
