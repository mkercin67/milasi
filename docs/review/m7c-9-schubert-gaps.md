# M7c-9c Schubert D. 547 "An die Musik" (mm. 1-5): differences and open points

Source: IMSLP #10425, Breitkopf & Haertel Gesamtausgabe 1895 (Public Domain). All claims UNVERIFIED until the owner checks them against that scan.

- Reading aid: the notes were read with a second, cleanly typeset edition (IMSLP #234875, non-commercial licence) kept only in tests/private/ as a local visual reference, as CORPUS_POLICY section 2 allows. It is not cited as a source, nothing of it is committed, and the owner checks the result against #10425.
- The start repeat sign before m. 3 is entered (the owner spotted it); the matching end repeat lies after the excerpt, so the playback does not repeat here.
- Lyrics: verse 1 stops its extender at "en" (the last two notes of m. 5 carry no syllable); verse 2 gives them "flos-" "sen,". The syllables of the edition after m. 5 are not entered.
- Grace note: the small eighth B4 in front of the first note of m. 5 is entered as an appoggiatura (the scan shows no slash).
- The edition has 5 measures in its first system (this fixture); the piano chords are repeated eighth-note chords read note by note, see tests SC2.
- Open: m. 4, right hand, chords 5 and 6 (F#3 with D4 and F#4, then F#3 with F#4 only).
- m. 2: the short diminuendo fork under the first two chords is in the Breitkopf scan only (the owner spotted it); entered as a hairpin from chord 1 to chord 2.
